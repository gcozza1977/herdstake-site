The pattern

What the cases have in common

01

Paper ran ahead of the pens

The cattle lived on invoices, borrowing-base reports and investor statements long after they were gone, or before they were ever bought.

02

The tell was physical

Each scheme ended when someone counted or looked: a processor's check of the lots, a lender's inspection, a receiver's count.

03

Records alone don't see empty pens

Lien and permit checks find a second lender or a claim bigger than the yard. They don't count cattle. That's why our full checks add a dated satellite image, and why we say so when a physical count is the next step.
