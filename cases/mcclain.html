McClain Farms and 7M Cattle Feeders

What happened

McClain Farms, a Kentucky cattle business, and two Texas Panhandle feedyards under the same ownership, 7M Cattle Feeders and McClain Feed Yard near Hereford and Friona, borrowed against cattle from Rabo AgriFinance and raised money from investors. DTN reported that 7M was a yard built for 115,000 head and McClain Feed Yard about 3,000.

According to court filings reported by Beef and the Southwest Ledger, the December 2022 borrowing-base report listed 89,522 head of cattle and a $97.2 million borrowing base. By February 2023 the businesses themselves reported 37,992 head and $36.7 million. On April 4, 2023, Rabo's collateral inspection found 7,481 head in Texas and 3,094 in Kentucky: 10,575 in all.

The owner died in April 2023, and on April 28 the three companies filed Chapter 7 bankruptcy in the Northern District of Texas. Rabo says it is owed about $50.6 million, and more than 100 investors were caught up as well. The fallout is still in court: the bankruptcy trustee has sued several banks, investors filed a class action in 2025 against Community Financial Services Bank, Rabo AgriFinance and Mechanics Bank over their alleged roles, and the case became the first test of the livestock Dealer Trust.

What caught it

A physical count. The lender's April 2023 inspection found roughly one head for every eight the December report had listed.

Which check would have caught it sooner

A capacity check alone would have passed it. 89,522 head is well under what a 115,000-head yard can hold, so a records screen's capacity test would not have flagged the December report.

The borrower's own numbers were the first alarm. Reported head fell 58% in two months. A drop that size in a borrowing base is a reason to count, not just to re-margin.

A dated image would have shown it. In April about 7,500 head were in Texas yards built for roughly 118,000: around 6% full. Empty pens on that scale show plainly from above. A lien search would also have shown other secured parties, but it doesn't count cattle.
