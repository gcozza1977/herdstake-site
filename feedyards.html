Fewer site counts for honest yards. Help us prove it.

We count cattle from satellite images so lenders can check a borrower without another visit. Before we sell checks at scale, we're testing our counts against five real yards.

The blind test

What we ask.

  • We count your pens from satellite images on a few past dates, and send you our numbers before we see yours.
  • You share your pen sheets, or total head on feed, for those same dates.
  • One 15-minute call to walk through your pen map.
  • If we both want to, a few more dates over the next three months.
