The problem

Cattle collateral is still checked by eye.

A few site visits a year leave long gaps. The largest cattle frauds lived in those gaps: herds that existed only on paper, or the same cattle pledged twice.

What we check

Four checks on every borrower and yard.

Head count

Counted from 30 cm satellite imagery and given as a range, with the image date.

Claim vs. capacity

The borrower's claimed head set against the yard's permitted capacity.

Other liens

Other pledges on the same cattle, from UCC filings and the state Clear Title lists.

Yard records

The yard's Packers & Stockyards registration and any court records.

How it works

A list in, a signed report out.

Nothing to integrate and no credit files. You send a short list, and we do the rest.

  1. You send a listBorrower, yard and claimed head. No access to your systems.
  2. We check each yardSatellite imagery, permits, lien filings and yard records.
  3. You get a dated reportIn your state's inspection format, plus a spreadsheet, reviewed and signed by a qualified ag credit reviewer.
  4. Gaps get a physical countIf a count falls outside the range, a brand inspector or vet counts the cattle within 10 days.
