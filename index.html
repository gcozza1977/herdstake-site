What we check

Four checks on every borrower. One report.

01 / COUNT

Head count

Counted from 30 cm satellite imagery and given as a range, with the image date.

02 / CAPACITY

Claim vs. capacity

The borrower's claimed head set against the yard's permitted capacity.

03 / LIENS

Other pledges

Other liens on the same cattle, from UCC filings and the state Clear Title lists.

04 / RECORDS

Yard records

The yard's Packers & Stockyards registration and any court records.

How it works

A list in. A signed report out.

No integration and no credit files. You send a short list, and we do the rest.

  1. 01
    You send a listBorrower, yard and claimed head. Nothing touches your systems.
  2. 02
    We check each yardSatellite imagery, permits, lien filings and yard records.
  3. 03
    You get a dated reportIn your state's inspection format, plus a spreadsheet, reviewed and signed by a qualified ag credit reviewer.
  4. 04
    Gaps get a physical countIf a count falls outside the range, a brand inspector or vet counts within 10 days.
