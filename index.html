What we check, for each borrower and yard

Head count

Counted from 30 cm satellite imagery, given as a range with the image date.

Claim vs. capacity

The borrower's claimed head set against the yard's permitted capacity.

Other liens

Other pledges on the same cattle, from UCC filings and the state Clear Title lists.

Yard records

The yard's Packers & Stockyards registration and any court records.

How it works

  1. You send a list: borrower, yard, claimed head. No access to your systems, no credit files.
  2. We check each yard from imagery and records.
  3. You get a dated report in your state's inspection format, plus a spreadsheet, reviewed and signed by a qualified ag credit reviewer.
  4. If a count falls outside the range, a brand inspector or vet counts the cattle within 10 days.

What it isn't: a guarantee, an appraisal or a replacement for site visits. It's a documented check between visits, built for the independence, dating and documentation that examiners look for.
