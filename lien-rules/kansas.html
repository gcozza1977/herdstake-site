Lien rules by state · Kansas

Kansas cattle lien rules.

Direct notice. Kansas is not on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system.

System

Direct notice. Kansas is not on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system.

What a buyer must do

A buyer of cattle in the ordinary course takes free of the lender's lien unless, within 1 year before the sale, it received written notice meeting 7 U.S.C. § 1631(e)(1) and then failed to meet the payment terms in it. Kansas's own UCC rule for buyers in the ordinary course (K.S.A. 84-9-320(a)) expressly leaves out buyers of farm products from people engaged in farming, so the federal rule governs those sales. Sale barns selling for others: § 1631(g).

Searching financing statements

UCC financing statements are filed with the Kansas Secretary of State. Search steps and fees: not yet confirmed by Herdstake; see the Secretary of State's UCC page.

Brand inspection

Brand inspection is not mandatory in Kansas. The Kansas Department of Agriculture has six contract brand inspectors who inspect on request for a fee and serve six livestock markets that contract for inspection. Brands must be registered with the Division of Animal Health Brand Office before use.

State offices

Kansas Secretary of State, UCC · KDA brands program
