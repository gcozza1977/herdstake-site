Lien rules by state · Missouri

Missouri cattle lien rules.

Direct notice. Missouri is not on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system, so a buyer is bound by a lender's lien only if it received the written notice the federal rule describes.

System

Direct notice. Missouri does not appear among the 19 states on USDA's list of certified central filing systems, so the federal direct-notice rule applies (7 U.S.C. § 1631(e)(1)).

What a buyer must do

A buyer of cattle takes free of the lender's security interest unless, within one year before the sale, it received written notice from the lender (or the seller) that meets § 1631(e)(1) and did not then make payment as the notice directs, for example jointly to the seller and the lender (§ 1631(e)(1)(B)). Sale barns selling for others: § 1631(g). Missouri's UCC (RSMo § 400.9-320) leaves a person buying farm products from a person engaged in farming operations out of its ordinary buyer-in-the-course rule, so the federal rule governs. Missouri's chapter on statutory liens against personal property (RSMo ch. 430) includes liens for keeping animals (for example § 430.150); we did not read their text or how they rank against a lender, so check them with counsel.

Searching financing statements

Financing statements are filed with the Missouri Secretary of State's UCC Division. Its fee page lists a paper UCC-1 at $17 plus $1 per attachment page, online filings at $10 per filing plus a convenience fee, and a UCC-11 search at $27 for all plus $1 per page for copies. Financing statements are effective five years and may be continued for five more. Confirm current fees on the Secretary of State's UCC page before you rely on them.

Brand inspection

We found no mandatory brand inspection in the Missouri sources we read. Missouri keeps a brand registry: a livestock brand must be registered with the Missouri Department of Agriculture's Division of Animal Health before any animal is branded (Marks and Brands of Animal Law, chapter 268). Fees listed: $35 to register, $20 maintenance, $10 to transfer, and $15 for the Brand Book; the department reports about 5,000 recorded brands.

State offices

Missouri Secretary of State, UCC Division (573-751-4628) · Missouri Department of Agriculture, brand registry (Animal Health, 573-751-3377)
