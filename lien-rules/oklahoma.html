Lien rules by state · Oklahoma

Oklahoma cattle lien rules.

Central filing. Oklahoma is on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system (products covered: "All"). Since March 16, 2026 it is run by the Oklahoma County Clerk.

System

Central filing. USDA approved the transfer of Oklahoma's central filing system from the Secretary of State to the Oklahoma County Clerk, applicable March 16, 2026 (Federal Register doc. 2026-05095); effective financing statements (EFS) can be filed electronically and are posted online within 48 hours of recording. The statute is 12A O.S. § 1-9-320.6, which says the Oklahoma County Clerk shall develop and maintain the central filing system.

What a buyer must do

A buyer of cattle takes subject to the lender's lien if it failed to register with the state's central filing system before buying and the lender had filed an effective financing statement (EFS) covering the cattle (7 U.S.C. § 1631(e)(2)), or if it received the state's written notice naming the seller and product and did not secure a waiver or release (§ 1631(e)(3)). Sale barns selling for others: § 1631(g). An EFS does not replace the lender's UCC financing statement. Under § 1-9-320.6, a buyer's registration lasts one year from its effective date and lists the farm products and counties it covers (or all 77 counties), and the master list goes out monthly, on or before the last business day of each month. 28 O.S. § 111 sets a $50 yearly registration fee, waived if the master list is downloaded online, and $150 to $400 a year for master-list copies depending on counties and products; that section still names the Secretary of State, so confirm current fees with the County Clerk.

Searching financing statements

Most UCC filings in Oklahoma are filed with the Oklahoma County Clerk, and farm-products filings must be made in its central filing office (320 Robert S. Kerr Ave, Oklahoma City; 405-270-0082). The County Clerk's 2025 administrative rules set UCC filing at $10 (paper, 1 to 5 pages, then $1 per extra page; electronic $10) and UCC search at $10 per debtor name. 28 O.S. § 111 sets the EFS filing fee at $10 and a written confirmation of EFS on a debtor at $6.

Brand inspection

Brand registration in Oklahoma is handled by the Oklahoma Cattlemen's Association's brand division (2 O.S. § 2-4-5), and the association says state registration of a brand is not required by law ($60 for a five-year cycle). We did not find a state brand-inspection requirement for cattle sales in the sources we read; check with the buyer or sale barn.
