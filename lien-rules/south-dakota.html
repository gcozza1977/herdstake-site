Lien rules by state · South Dakota

South Dakota cattle lien rules.

Central filing. South Dakota is on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system (certified September 29, 1987; products covered: "All*"), run by the Secretary of State.

System

Central filing. USDA lists South Dakota as certified on September 29, 1987, with products covered shown as "All*"; USDA's page does not explain the asterisk. The Secretary of State runs the central agricultural filing system (SDCL 57A-9-525.2 and 57A-9-525.3), and a lender may file a combined effective financing statement (EFS) and UCC financing statement on one form (SDCL 57A-9-530).

What a buyer must do

A buyer of cattle takes subject to the lender's lien if it failed to register with the state's central filing system before buying and the lender had filed an EFS covering the cattle (7 U.S.C. § 1631(e)(2)), or if it received the state's written notice naming the seller and product and did not secure a waiver or release (§ 1631(e)(3)). Sale barns selling for others: § 1631(g). South Dakota's UCC (SDCL 57A-9-320) leaves buyers of farm products from farmers out of its ordinary buyer rule. SDCL 57A-9-525.4 sets the yearly registration fee for the livestock EFS master list at $120; the Secretary of State's fee schedule lists "EFS Crop/Livestock - Monthly" at $20. We did not find, in the statutes or rules we read, how often the master list goes out or the registration steps: ask the Secretary of State.

Searching financing statements

Search and file at the Secretary of State's UCC portal. By statute (SDCL 57A-9-525), a certificate showing whether any financing statement names a debtor costs $10 by internet or $20 in writing, and filing an EFS or UCC-1 costs $20 by internet or $25 on paper for one page, plus $3 per extra debtor name. The Secretary of State's fee schedule lists the UCC-2 information request at $10 electronic or $20 paper per debtor, search-only database access at $300 a year and copies at $2 per page (the statute says $1 per page).

Brand inspection

Yes, in the brand inspection area: the 22 counties named in SDCL 40-20-1, which the Brand Board describes as all counties west of the Missouri River. Selling or transferring ownership of livestock in that area without an ownership inspection is a Class 2 misdemeanor (SDCL 40-20-26.1), as is removing livestock from the area without one (SDCL 40-20-4); a certificate is good for 24 hours, and inspection is required whether or not the cattle are branded. The fee is $1.45 per head for cattle from September 30, 2026 (ARSD 12:10:02:05; the Board's own fee page still shows $1.00), plus mileage, and the owner or seller pays it before clearance is delivered (SDCL 40-21-7). Brand registration with the Brand Board: $40 application and $50 per location per species (ARSD 12:10:01:16, 12:10:01:12).

State offices

South Dakota Secretary of State, UCC and EFS portal · South Dakota Brand Board (605-773-3324)
