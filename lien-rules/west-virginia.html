Lien rules by state · West Virginia

West Virginia cattle lien rules.

Central filing. West Virginia is on USDA's list of states with a certified central filing system.

System

Central filing. West Virginia is on the list of 19 states USDA's Agricultural Marketing Service gives as having a certified central filing system, with products covered shown as "All" (list as Herdstake read it for the index page; USDA's page refused our request on November 25, 2026, so it was not re-read).

What a buyer must do

Under 7 U.S.C. § 1631(e)(2) and (e)(3), a buyer takes subject to the lien if it did not register with the state's central filing system before buying and the lender had filed an effective financing statement covering the product, or if it received the state's written notice naming the seller and product and did not get a waiver or release, for example by paying as required. Sale barns selling for others: § 1631(g). Herdstake has not confirmed the steps for registering as a buyer in West Virginia: ask the Secretary of State before buying.

Searching financing statements

A lender whose collateral includes farm property files a UCC financing statement with the Secretary of State. The Secretary of State's UCC Division takes filings through its online services system and offers online UCC search requests. Search fees: not yet confirmed by Herdstake.
