How a Herdstake records screen works.

What we check, where we look, how a finding is worded, and what the screen does not do. Updated November 28, 2026.

What it is

A dated check of public records and the lender's own records for each livestock borrower on a list. Every line of the report names the source and the date it was searched. A person on our team reviews each report before it goes out. Delivery is within five business days of the list.

What it is not

The screen does not confirm that cattle are physically present, and it does not value them. A physical count is a separate service: we confirm local availability, timing and cost with the lender before anything is ordered. A head-count range from a dated satellite image is planned for Q1 2027 and is not available today.

What we need from the lender

One row per borrower: legal name, yard name, yard county and state, claimed head and the as-of date. Optionally the buyer list or notice log, for the buyer-notice check, and any yard inventory statements the lender already receives. No system access and no credit files. Download the list template. How a result is worded

Each borrower gets one of three results, with the reason and a next step beside it.

Clear. Nothing that doesn't reconcile.
Follow up. Something to confirm, such as another lender's UCC filing or a buyer notice more than a year old.
Flag. A finding that bears on the claim itself, such as a claim above the yard's permitted capacity.

We report what the records show. A gap or a filing is not an accusation, and the report says so when it may have an ordinary cause: cattle shipped since the borrower's report, cattle at another yard, or a filing that doesn't cover cattle at this yard.

Independence

The lender orders and pays, which keeps the screen independent of the borrower. Herdstake has no interest in the borrower or the yard. Reports go only to the ordering lender, never to another lender, the borrower or the yard.

Coverage

Records screens are available now for Texas, Kansas and Nebraska. Other states: ask, and we'll say whether we can cover them before you order.

Limits

Public records can lag the real world: a lien filed last week may not be searchable yet, and a court filing may be indexed late. The report is a monitoring report for the ordering lender's own use. It is not a guarantee, an appraisal, a title or legal opinion or a consumer report, and no one else may rely on it. Lien and notice rules differ by state; see the lien rules, which are general information and not legal advice.

When the satellite range launches

A satellite image cannot count head by head with perfect accuracy, so every count will be a range, shown with the image date and image ID. A claim above the range gets a closer look and, where warranted, a physical count by an independent counter, local veterinarian or brand inspector. The sample report shows the format: sample report.
