3 · What this means and next steps

Prairie Example Feeders. The claim is more than the yard is allowed to hold, so some of the cattle may be at another yard, already shipped, or not there. Ask the yard for its inventory statement for the borrower's lots, or order a physical count; Herdstake quotes the count before it's ordered. Ask the borrower about the feed supplier's suit: in some states a supplier's lien can rank ahead of the bank's. Ask the ACH buyer to confirm how sale proceeds will reach the bank.

Example Cattle Co. Confirm whether the other lender's UCC filing covers cattle at this yard, and renew the notice to the third listed buyer.

Sandhill Example Ranch. No action.
