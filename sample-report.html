3 · What this means

Our range covers the cattle standing in open pens on Nov 4. A gap of this size can have ordinary causes: cattle shipped since Oct 31, cattle held at another yard, or a borrower's report not yet updated. This report doesn't say why the gap exists. A physical count settles it.

The other lender's UCC filing may or may not cover cattle at this yard; its secured party is named in the attached file.

The notice to the third buyer is more than a year old, so cattle sold to that buyer may not carry the bank's lien until a new notice goes out.

4 · Recommended next step

A physical count by an independent counter or a local veterinarian, or a brand inspector where the state has one. Herdstake confirms local availability, timing and cost before it's ordered; the bank pays the counter's fee at cost. Separately, renew the notice to the third listed buyer.
