The rules

What a Texas infested zone restricts

  • No warm-blooded animal leaves an infested zone without prior authorization from the Texas Animal Health Commission (TAHC).
  • Before a move, the owner schedules an inspection by TAHC or a TAHC-certified screwworm inspector.
  • Animals are treated or given preventive treatment, and the inspector's movement certificate names the product used.
  • The certificate, with each animal's official ID, travels with the animals.

Zones are set, changed and released by executive director orders, often within weeks, so the TAHC zone map is the live record. New Mexico and Arizona run their own zones; USDA links their maps from its current-status page.

For lenders

What it means for your loans

FEEDYARD COLLATERAL

Panhandle, Kansas, Nebraska

No detections in these feeding areas as of November 11. The exposure is cattle placed from zone counties: ask for their movement certificates, and watch whether detections move north.

COW-CALF, STOCKER, SMALL RUMINANTS

West and South Texas, southern NM, Arizona

Animals inside a zone can't leave the premises until they're inspected and cleared, which can push back sales and repayment. Treatment adds cost, and untreated infestations can kill animals. Know which borrowers sit in or next to a zone.

MEXICAN FEEDER CATTLE

Ports reopening in phases

USDA's announced dates: Douglas, Ariz., Aug 24; Santa Teresa, N.M., Sept 24; Columbus, N.M., Oct 26; Nogales, Ariz., Nov 30, 2026. Imported cattle come in under USDA's import protocol.
