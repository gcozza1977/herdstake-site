Security & data handling

How we handle your borrower list.

For your vendor review. Plain answers about what we ask for, what we do with it and who else touches it. Updated November 28, 2026.

What we ask you to send

One row per borrower: legal name, yard name, yard county and state, claimed head and the as-of date. Optionally the buyer list or notice log and any yard inventory statements you already receive. We don't need credit files, loan documents, account numbers or individuals' Social Security numbers, and ask you not to send them.

How to send it

By email to team@herdstake.com, using the list template. Please don't put borrower details in the contact form on this website. The form collects only your lender or company name, work email and approximate volume.

What we do with it

We use the list only to prepare your records screen. We search public records and use your notice log for the buyer-notice check. We don't need access to any of your systems. Reports go only to you, never to another lender, the borrower or the yard. We don't sell your list or use it to market to your borrowers.

Deleting your data

Ask us at any time to delete your list and the files we made from it, and we'll confirm by email when it's done.

Who else is involved

This website is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Visit counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect your borrower data. The website's contact form is delivered by FormSubmit. Borrower lists don't pass through any of these when sent by email as above.

Vendor review

If your vendor-management policy needs a questionnaire answered, email us and we'll answer it in writing.
