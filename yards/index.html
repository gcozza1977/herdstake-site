Permitted feedyards, by state and county.

225 commercial cattle feeding operations with a permitted capacity of 1,000 head or more, each with its permit and the state record it comes from. Kansas (225).

What's here: the business name as it appears on the permit, the county, the permitted capacity, and the permit number, linked to the state's own record. What isn't: permits held in an individual's name, street addresses, and anything about how a yard is run. Permitted capacity is the most a permit allows, not a count of cattle on feed. More states are being added.
